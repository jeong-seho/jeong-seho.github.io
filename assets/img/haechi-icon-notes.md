# Haechi favicon artwork

The previous horned-lion SVG has been replaced by a new front-facing Haechi illustration based on the facial proportions of the Gwanghwamun stone guardian. The original artwork was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, followed by a cleanup edit. Raster exports preserve the generated alpha; no reference photograph is included in this repository.

## Saved assets

- `haechi-icon-source.png`: selected generated source, 1254 × 1254.
- `favicon.png`: 192 × 192 transparent browser icon.
- `../../favicon.ico`: browser fallback with 16, 32 and 48 px PNG frames.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 × 180, opaque white exterior.

The browser consumes the small exports, not the large source. The superseded SVG is no longer linked or shipped.

## Reference credit

Subject / morphology reference: **[해태상] 한국민족문화대백과사전, 한국학중앙연구원**.

- [Article and photograph credits](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0062778)
- [Reference photograph](https://devin.aks.ac.kr/image/acaccc5f-67d0-4337-8ceb-9ab99779f251?preset=orig)

The reference was consulted for the cloud-scroll forehead, broad nostril-bearing nose, continuous upper lip, short incisors and tapered beard. The photograph's pixels, background and watermark are not used as website assets. This is not the Seoul city mascot or an official city symbol.
